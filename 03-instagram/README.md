# Task 3 | Instagram engagement

[Executed notebook](analysis.ipynb) · [One-page content calendar and five strategies](strategy.pdf)

Seven related tables describe 100 users, 257 posts, 8,782 likes and 7,488 comments. The notebook counts likes and comments separately for each post and normalizes them by the author's incoming follower count.

Video and carousel posts both have a median of 82.89 interactions per 100 followers. Photos have a median of 81.82. All posts share a single timestamp, and the follow table is a snapshot. The strategy document therefore proposes a four-week timing test for Alfido Tech. It also explains how to measure follower growth on the account.

To run offline, save the original archive as `data/instgram.zip` beside the notebook. The spelling matches the Kaggle dataset identifier. [Kaggle source](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/bhanupratapbiswas/instgram)
